People call us every week asking this, often a little embarrassed to ask. You should not be. A price you can plan for is part of good care.

## Our prices

A new-patient exam, x-rays and cleaning is $185 at Linden. A returning check-up and clean is $165. Kids under 12 are $120. Those are the full prices, with nothing added at the desk.

## Why prices vary

Across Portland you will see anything from about $150 to $400 for the same visit. The difference usually comes down to which x-rays are included, whether the cleaning is billed separately, and how long the appointment is. Ask any practice for the total, including x-rays, before you book.

## The membership plan

If you do not have insurance, our membership costs $29 a month for adults and $19 for kids. It covers two check-ups and cleanings a year, all routine x-rays, one emergency exam, and 15 percent off any treatment. Most members save money in the first year.

## What we promise

You will always get the price before treatment, in writing. If something changes partway through, we stop and tell you.
