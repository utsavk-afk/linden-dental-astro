Most people brush for about 45 seconds. Two minutes feels long until you time it, and then it feels very long.

## Use a timer you do not have to watch

A two-minute song works better than a sand timer. Electric brushes with a 30-second buzz are even better, because they also tell you when to move to the next quarter of your mouth.

## Brush together

Kids copy. If they see you brush at the same time, they brush longer. It also means you can check their back teeth, which is where most kids’ cavities show up.

## Keep the brush where they can see it

Out of sight is out of mind. A cup by the sink, at their height, does a surprising amount of the work.

## Do not rinse after

Spit, do not rinse. Rinsing washes away the fluoride that keeps working after you stop brushing. It is the easiest change on this list.
