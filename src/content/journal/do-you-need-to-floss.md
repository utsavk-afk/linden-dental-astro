Short answer: you need to clean between your teeth. Floss is one way to do it, and for a lot of people it is not the best one.

## Why between the teeth matters

Your toothbrush cleans about 60 percent of each tooth's surface. The rest is the sides, where teeth touch. That is where most adult cavities and nearly all gum disease start.

## If you hate flossing

Try interdental brushes. They are tiny bottle brushes that slide between teeth, and most patients find them faster and easier than floss. We will show you which sizes fit your gaps at your next check-up.

Water flossers are another option, especially if you have braces, bridges or implants. They are less thorough than brushes but far better than nothing.

## How often

Once a day, any time. Before bed is ideal, but the best time is whichever one you will stick to.

## What we will not do

Lecture you about it. If you are not cleaning between your teeth now, starting with three nights a week is a real improvement, and we will notice it at your next visit.
