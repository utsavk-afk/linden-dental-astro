faqs:
  - q: How often should I come in?
    a: Every six months suits most adults and kids. If your gums need closer watching we may suggest every three or four months, and we will explain why.
  - q: Will my insurance cover it?
    a: Most PPO plans cover two check-ups a year in full. June checks your plan before your visit and tells you if anything is not covered.
  - q: Do I have to have x-rays?
    a: No. We recommend them once a year because they show problems between teeth that we cannot see. You can always say no.

A check-up at Linden takes an hour, which is longer than most practices book. That extra time is where the explaining happens.

We do not lecture. If we spot something, we show you on the screen, tell you whether it needs doing now or can be watched, and give you the price before anything is scheduled.
